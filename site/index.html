Open Vitæ never sends applications or chooses consent for you. You review the facts, decide what to say and make the final connection.

Use AI
on your terms.

Connect a supported service, take a prompt to the AI you prefer, or prepare your materials yourself. Your profile, saved jobs and drafts work without an AI connection or internet access.

ChatGPT connects through the official Codex sign-in flow. Claude and Gemini use API keys with separate billing or project quotas; their consumer subscriptions are not connected.

Keep your work
in your workspace.

Your profile, jobs and documents are saved on your computer. Relevant context goes to your chosen AI service when you ask for help. Review any prompt before sharing it elsewhere.

No Open Vitæ account or telemetry. Export your data, keep a backup and inspect the source code whenever you want.

Before you begin.

Does it apply for me?
No. You review the materials and send the application yourself. Mark it as applied afterward to update your tracker.
What works offline?
Your profile, CV imports, saved jobs, manual job entry, drafts, prompts and backups. Finding new jobs online or asking an AI service for help requires an internet connection.
What does it cost?
Open Vitæ has no paywall. Optional AI use is subject to your service’s account limits, API charges or project quota.
Where do the jobs come from?
Public feeds from Himalayas and Arbeitnow, company boards you add from Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby, and your own links or descriptions. Coverage is partial. Check the opening and eligibility with the employer.
Will it run on my Mac?
The current scope is macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. Intel Macs are not supported.
Can I download it now?
A verified public installer is not available yet. You can follow releases on GitHub or run the preview from source. A download will appear here after signing, notarization and checks on a clean Mac.
